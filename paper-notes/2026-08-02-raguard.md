## Production issue

RAG systems can trust poisoned retrieved passages and answer with fabricated or adversary-steered claims.

## Core claim

Combine retriever hardening with a leave-one-out inference filter that removes retrieved documents whose presence destabilizes the model's answer.

## What I would implement

Borrow the evaluation shape before the method: build a small poisoning harness for one repo retrieval slice, then test whether a cheap counterfactual filter catches harmful retrieved chunks.

## What would have to be true

I need an actual corpus-poisoning benchmark inside this repo, dense retrieval must be part of the threat model, and the extra inference passes must be affordable for the target workflow.

## What I would measure

Attack success rate reduction versus clean-answer regression, plus the added latency and token cost from the leave-one-out filtering step.

## Failure modes / reasons to reject

The paper's strongest result depends on multiple extra generator passes per query, which is expensive, and its threat model is narrower than the broader prompt-injection surface in this repo.

## Verdict

`BORROW`

## What changes the verdict

Upgrade to `BUILD` if Sprint 09 or a future RAG safety slice produces a real poisoning harness that this defense can improve. Downgrade to `PARK` later if the repo stays focused on other safety failure modes.
